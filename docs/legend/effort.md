# Reasoning effort

`low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh` or `max`: how deeply the model thinks, and with it how many
tokens a turn spends. `/effort` changes it at once, and the line follows.

Higher effort can lower the total cost of long, agentic work (fewer turns); on short tasks it
is spend you don't need.
